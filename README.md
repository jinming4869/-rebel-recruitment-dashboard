## Weak-state intelligence handoff

The [14 October 2026 research handoff release](https://github.com/jinming4869/-rebel-recruitment-dashboard/releases/tag/weak-state-intelligence-handoff-2026-10-14) provides the English-language data and design package. [Download the ZIP directly](https://github.com/jinming4869/-rebel-recruitment-dashboard/releases/download/weak-state-intelligence-handoff-2026-10-14/Weak_State_Intelligence_Handoff_2026-10-14.zip) (141,491,322 bytes; SHA-256 `7b0e6cfd142eff6c95b6a928ac747f16c0cea17a7225bb6bcf72a4654ad5e1a8`).

**Use the research branch for study code and the Release asset for source files.** The package includes `README.md`, `WITHIN_COUNTRY_DESIGN.md`, `DATA_MAP.md`, a source manifest, checksums, and the relevant datasets and codebooks. Its central empirical unit is a government–claimant bargaining episode, with local evidence nested inside it. The national series support the denominator and descriptive checks; they do not reveal private government beliefs or first offers.

The research pipeline has not yet run on the released inputs. After downloading, read the [study README](https://github.com/jinming4869/-rebel-recruitment-dashboard/blob/claude/civil-war-research-ch0ohr/research/weak_state_intelligence/README.md) and register the sources under that project's source IDs. Keep the feasibility gates pending until the archival and case evidence has been collected.
