01 · RECRUITMENT POOL

Who constitutes the potential manpower pool?

Background 与 social composition 只能说明哪些人出现在已观察到的 participant sample 中；它们本身不能识别 Exposure、Selection，也不能直接当作 recruitment rate。

Three margins, one background matrix

Positive values mean the first named group has more of the row characteristic.

Method & risk set

Inverse-probability weighted standardized differences. Entry compares ex-combatants with the designed noncombatant comparison; faction compares RUF with CDF composition; recruitment form compares RUF abductees with RUF volunteers. Faction membership is not treated as voluntary choice.

Interpretation

This is weighted sample composition, not a population recruitment rate. The noncombatant frame is a designed comparison sample.

02 · EXPOSURE

Who is actually exposed to recruitment efforts or organizational reach?

现有 micro evidence 主要观察 contact、participation 或 abduction 之后的人。它能呈现 age-conditioned pattern，却不能提供衡量 organizational reach 所需的总体 denominator。

关键概念：Exposure 不能由“recruitability”反推。Age 与 background 可能影响接触机会，但当前 sample 无法识别所有可能被组织触达的人。

03 · ORGANIZATIONAL DEMAND

What kinds and quantities of manpower does the organization need?

这是一个独立的 organizational question。当前 archive 更善于测量 recruitment instruments 与部分 ex post roles，却缺少 manpower requirement、vacancy、replacement need 和 desired skill composition。

Observed indirectly

Roles after entry

SWAY records weapons, duties, threats, beliefs, and duration after abduction. These outcomes may reflect assignment, but they are not prior statements of organizational demand.

Do not substitute

Capacity is not demand

Territorial control, external support, conflict intensity, and organizational institutions condition recruitment; none directly measures how many or what kinds of recruits were sought.

Evidence gap

Demand remains undermeasured

No current source provides a consistent cross-organizational series of required headcount, replacement pressure, role vacancies, quotas, or desired recruit attributes.

05 · SELECTION / SCREENING

Who is selected, accepted, targeted, exempted, or excluded?

Selection 在分析上不同于初始 Recruitment Pool，也不同于组织使用的 recruitment instruments。现有 source 更常描述已经进入组织的人，而不是 screening decision 本身。

Available evidence

Composition after selection

H&W background margins and child-soldier shares show who appears among observed recruits or organizations. They do not reveal the full candidate pool.

Partial signals

Targeting and exemption

Age patterns, coercive categories, and case-specific accounts can suggest targeting, but they do not consistently distinguish acceptance, exemption, refusal, escape, or exclusion.

Evidence gap

No screening denominator

The archive lacks comparable applicant, contacted-person, acceptance, rejection, exemption, and role-allocation counts across organizations.

06 · INCORPORATION

How are recruits trained, assigned, disciplined, socialized, and integrated?

Role assignment、weapons、indoctrination、loyalty、written rules、tribunals 与 sanctions 都属于 entry 之后的 organizational process，不能与 recruitment instrument 混为一谈。
